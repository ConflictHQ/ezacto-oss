the ones that would otherwise be forgotten between two conversations.

## Convention

- Each question has a **stable ID** (`q-001`, `q-002`, …). Never reuse one.
- A question keeps its ID as it moves between the three sections below.
- When a question is answered and the answer changes what people do, write the
  decision in `decisions.md` and link the two.
